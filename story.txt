chapter 1: The King's Call

The kingdom was in danger.
King Kay Kavus called Rostam and asked him to begin a great journey.
Rostam accepted the mission and prepared his horse Rakhsh for the seven trials

Chapter 2: Rostam Starts His Journey

Rostam left the kingdom with his loyal horse Rakhsh.
The hero entered the dangerous road of the seven trials.

Chapter 3: The First Trial - The Lion

A powerful lion attacked Rakhsh during the night.
Rakhsh fought bravely and Rostam continued his journey.
